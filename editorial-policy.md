## What are our sourcing rules?

We publish from primary documents only. If a claim cannot be traced to a document a regulator, court or standard-setter actually issued, it does not go on this site.

| Source | Status |
|---|---|
| [PVARA official]( https://pvara.org ) | Primary. Cited and linked |
| SECP notifications and rules | Primary. Cited |
| State Bank of Pakistan circulars | Primary. Cited |
| FBR guidance and ordinances | Primary. Cited |
| Pakistani court judgments | Primary. Cited |
| FATF standards and guidance | Primary. Cited |
| News coverage, social media, forums | Not used as a source |
| Anonymous or unverified tips | Not published without a document behind them |

Where a source document exists online, we link to it. Where it exists only on paper or behind a portal, we name it precisely enough that a reader can request it.

## How is an article checked before it goes out?

Every article passes through the same sequence:

1. **Source retrieval.** A researcher obtains the primary document and reads it in full, not a summary of it.
2. **Drafting.** The analysis is written against the document, with the relevant section identified.
3. **Legal review.** Anything that states how a rule applies is reviewed by the lawyer on the team before publication.
4. **Editorial review.** Noor Aslam reviews and approves the piece.
5. **Publication.** The article is dated and the author is named.

## Do we use AI to write articles?

Yes, in part, and we would rather say so than have you find out.

Drafting is assisted by an AI language model working from a topic and source brief prepared by the research team. Every article is then reviewed by a person before it appears. Nothing publishes without that review.

What AI does not do here: it does not choose what we cover, it does not decide what a rule means, and it does not sign off a piece. Where an article states a legal position, a qualified lawyer on the team has checked it.

We take full responsibility for everything published under our name, regardless of how the first draft was produced.

## Who writes here?

Articles are published under **Noor Aslam**, Chief Executive Officer, who reviews and approves each one. The research behind them is done by the team described on the [about page]({{ '/about/' | relative_url }}): one qualified lawyer, three regulatory researchers with fifteen years of combined experience, and two operations staff.

We do not publish anonymous analysis and we do not use pen names.

## How do we handle corrections?

We would rather be corrected than be wrong quietly.

- **Report an error** to [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}). Corrections go to the front of the queue.
- **We respond within two business days**, whether or not we agree.
- **Material errors** — anything that would change what a reader does — are corrected in the article, with a dated correction note at the foot of the piece explaining what changed.
- **Minor errors** such as spelling or a broken link are fixed without a note.
- **We do not delete articles to hide a mistake.** If a piece is wrong beyond repair we mark it as withdrawn and say why.

## How do we stay independent?

Sarzif Policy earns money from one thing: when a client appoints a consultant we introduced, that client pays us 20% of the consultant's fee.

Note the direction. **The consultants are not our customers and never pay us anything.** That removes the conflict most referral sites carry, because there is no arrangement an adviser could offer us in exchange for coverage.

A conflict does remain, so here is how we contain it:

- **Consultants cannot buy coverage.** No article, mention or ranking on this site is for sale, at any price, to anyone.
- **Our fee is the same 20% whichever consultant a client appoints**, so we gain nothing by steering them toward an expensive adviser over a cheap one.
- **Reading this site is free and always will be.** Nothing is paywalled and nothing requires an enquiry.
- **Editorial and matching are separate.** What we publish is not influenced by who is on the panel, and being written about does not put a firm on it.
- **We accept no advertising, sponsored posts or paid links.**
- **No regulator funds, reviews or approves this site.**

If we ever write about a firm that is on our panel, we will say so in the article.

## What we are not

This site publishes information, not advice. We are not a law firm, not a licensed adviser, and not a substitute for one. Regulatory positions change, sometimes without notice.

Before you act on anything here, confirm it against the official position published by [PVARA](https://pvara.org) or take professional advice.
