# Images

## noor-aslam.jpg — author photo

Drop Noor's headshot here with **exactly** this filename:

    noor-aslam.jpg

It appears automatically in the author box at the bottom of every article,
and in the page's structured data for Google. Until the file exists, the
site shows the orange "NA" circle instead — nothing breaks either way.

**Requirements**
- Filename: `noor-aslam.jpg` (all lowercase, `.jpg`, not `.JPG` or `.jpeg`)
- Square crop, head and shoulders, face centred
- 400x400 pixels is plenty. Anything above 800x800 is wasted weight
- Under 200 KB. Compress at squoosh.app if it is larger
