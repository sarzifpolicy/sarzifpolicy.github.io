What is PVARA, and what is a VASP?

Two terms decide whether your business needs a licence in Pakistan. Here is what each one means.

What is PVARA?

PVARA is the Pakistan Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority. It is the government body that licenses and supervises crypto businesses in Pakistan. It decides the licence categories, the capital a firm must hold, and the anti-money-laundering duties it must follow.

What is a VASP?

A VASP is a Virtual Asset Service Provider. It is any business that exchanges, transfers, holds or safeguards crypto for other people. Exchanges, custodial wallets, brokers and crypto ATM operators are all VASPs and all need authorisation.

Who needs a licence?

Any firm offering crypto exchange, custody, broking or transfer services to customers in Pakistan. This covers local companies and foreign platforms that actively serve Pakistani users, even if the company is registered abroad.

What happens without one?

Operating without authorisation puts the firm outside the legal perimeter. That blocks banking access, exposes directors to enforcement, and makes it far harder to get licensed later once the regulator has a file on you.
