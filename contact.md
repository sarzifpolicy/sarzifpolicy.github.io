## How quickly do you reply?

Within **two business days**. Working week is Monday to Friday, Pakistan Standard Time.

If you have not heard back in that window, send the message again. It is more likely that something went to spam than that we ignored you.

## What do we want to hear about?

| Enquiry | Send it to us |
|---|---|
| **Licensing enquiry** | Use the form above. It is quicker |
| **Correction to an article** | Yes, and it goes to the front of the queue. Include the article and what is wrong |
| **Regulatory tip or document** | Yes. Tell us what you have and where it came from |
| **Question about a rule** | Yes, though we answer as information, never as legal advice |
| **You are a consultant wanting to join the panel** | Yes. Tell us which filings you have completed and in which countries |
| **Press or speaking request** | Yes |

## What we will not reply to

- Requests to help avoid or work around a regulator's requirements
- Offers to pay for coverage, a mention, a link or a higher ranking on our panel
- Bulk marketing and SEO outreach

## What happens to your email?

It goes to one inbox that the team reads. We use it to answer you, and for nothing else.

We do not sell, rent or share your details, and we do not add you to any list you did not ask for. If you contact us about a licence, we do not pass your details to any consultant until you tell us which one you want an introduction to.

Full detail is on the [privacy page]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}).

## Reporting an error

We publish a lot and we will occasionally get something wrong. If you spot it, tell us.

Send the article, the sentence, and what the correct position is. If a document supports your point, include it. We respond within two business days whether or not we agree, and material corrections are marked and dated on the article itself.

Our full [editorial policy]({{ '/editorial-policy/' | relative_url }}) sets out how this works.
